Exploring Europe · Lesson 3

The Great Plain Express

Zofia's ticket runs from the frozen lakes of Finland, across Russia and all the way to Poland. Halfway down it, in red, it says: “Brest: change of wheels.”

In your lesson, Zofia and her father ride the train from Helsinki to Saint Petersburg and Moscow, sail down the Volga, then cross the great plain west into Poland.

Why would a train need new wheels halfway across Europe, and why is Moscow's winter so much colder than Warsaw's?

There are five parts. Two of them start with a whole video (about 10 minutes each), and the rest takes about 20 minutes.

You can stop whenever you like: each part you finish is saved on this device. Zofia is from the story in your lesson. She's made up, but the places are real. Trains don't run between Finland and Russia today, so her trip is one people used to make.
