The Land of the Midnight Sun

Out fishing with her dad in the far north of Norway, Ingrid laughs: the sun hasn't set for weeks. How will she ever know when it's bedtime?

In your lesson, Ingrid fishes in a fjord, then crosses the mountains to visit her cousins in Sweden. Her summer is full of puzzles.

How can the sun shine at midnight, and why are two countries side by side so different?

There are five parts, about 25 minutes in all. You'll start by watching the Europe video again.

You can stop whenever you like: each part you finish is saved on this device. Ingrid is from the story in your lesson. She's made up, but the places are real.
